## Targeting

I'm aiming for Staff or Senior platform and backend roles: infrastructure, developer platforms, event-driven systems. I want individual-contributor scope, not management.

## Positioning

- For platform or infrastructure roles, lead with the deployment platform and the event pipeline.
- For logistics or supply-chain companies, lead with domain depth at Northwind.
- For Staff roles, put design docs, cross-team reviews, leading the platform team and mentoring up front.
- Treat the Brightleaf role as early-career context: one or two bullets.

## Tone

- Professional and confident. No fluff, no filler.
- Write like a senior engineer talking to a peer: clear, specific, technically credible.
- No corporate buzzwords or recruiter-speak.

## Bullets

- One line per bullet when possible, two at most.
- Pattern: action verb, what was built or done, then scale or impact.
- Prefer: Built, Designed, Architected, Implemented, Migrated, Optimized, Deployed, Led, Shipped, Automated, Reduced, Scaled, Integrated, Owned, Drove, Refactored, Instrumented.
- Avoid: Assisted, Helped, Participated, Contributed, Utilized, Supported, Handled, Spearheaded.
- Never use: synergy, detail-oriented, self-starter, results-driven, rockstar, ninja, guru, "proven track record", "team player", "passionate about technology", "wear many hats".
- Carry every number forward. If there's no number, show scope instead.
- Use precise technical terms. Match the Job Posting's vocabulary where my experience supports it.

## Length

- One page, and fill it. A sparse page is a missed opportunity.
- Three strong bullets per role beat five weak ones, but rephrase before cutting.

## Summary

- Two or three sentences. Start with role identity, never "I am".
- First sentence: who I am and my scope. Second: the strongest relevant achievement.

## Formatting

- Dates as "Mar 2022 – Present".
- Skills as a flat comma-separated list per category. No proficiency labels.
- Show a promotion as two titles under one employer heading.
- Independent projects go in Experience after the employers, only when they match the posting.
- No objective section, no "References available upon request".
